Design system

One plugin, used by people who are deaf, hard of hearing, blind, colour blind or new to forms, often on a cheap phone. Every token below comes from the plugin itself (customElements.get('db-form').design), so this page cannot drift from the real form.

  1. Nothing only by sound. Everything spoken is also shown as a caption; listening is a visible state; the beep also flashes.
  2. Nothing only by colour. Selected = check mark and heavier border. Errors = warning icon, bar and words.
  3. One accent. Neutral, warm surfaces. The accent marks the one thing to do next.
  4. Large, calm, left-aligned. 18 px base text, 48 px minimum targets, one question per page.
  5. Every script. Latin and Ge'ez are set together; Ge'ez gets more line height.

Typography

Atkinson Hyperlegible (Braille Institute) for the interface: letters that are hard to confuse (I l 1, 0 O). Noto Sans Ethiopic for Amharic and Tigrinya. Source Serif 4 only for this partner site's own headings, never inside the form.

Colour and themes

Six themes. People choose in the form's Colours menu; the choice is saved on their device and this site follows it. Contrast is calculated live from the tokens (WCAG 2.2: text 4.5:1, borders and icons 3:1).

Surfaces, radius and elevation

Three surfaces. Small radii: 4 px for controls, 8 px for choices and cards. One soft shadow, only on the form card.
