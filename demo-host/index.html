Join our programme

Free digital-skills classes in Bole. Sign up in a few minutes, by typing or by voice, in your own language.

Time
About 3 minutes, one question at a time.
You need
Nothing. No ID is needed to start.
Languages
English, አማርኛ, Afaan Oromoo, ትግርኛ, Soomaali.
Help
Call 0911 000 000, or visit us. Ethiopian Sign Language support on Thursdays.

Your support needs are optional and only seen by the organisers.

For partner developers

This page is not part of the plugin. It shows how any website adds the accessible form with two lines of HTML.

See the design system for colours, type and components.

Attributes
AttributeValuesWhat it does
flowsignup, job, employerWhich form to show.
langen, am, om, ti, soStarting language. The person can change it at any time.
lang-asknoSkip the first page that asks for the language.
themeauto, standard, dark, contrast, rg, byStarting colours. The person's own choice in the Colours menu wins.
apiURLWhere answers are sent.
audioURLFolder of read-aloud clips.
hidequestion idsLeave out optional questions.
